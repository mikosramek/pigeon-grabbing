### Camera

Used to take "photos" of objects, will be required for some quests.

This can use the game engine's frustrum culling to determine what objects are in view, and if the right object is, the player will get a photo in their inventory.

---

## Entities

### Pigeon Guide

A pigeon NPC that's modelled after Masha.
The player has to talk to her to progress through the first area, and then she'll give them hints as to what they can do.

### Pigeon Circle

This is a ground area that has pigeons in it. If the player gets too close, they'll fly away, but can be coaxed back to it by using seeds.

### Feather

A collectable feather, press `A` nearby to collect it
